How it works

Analysis in three steps

1
Add stocks to your watchlist
Search from 500+ Nifty 500 stocks and add the ones you follow. Your watchlist is private and syncs across devices via your Google account.
2
AI fetches live market data
One year of daily NSE price data is pulled from Yahoo Finance. Seven technical indicators are computed. Fundamental metrics are fetched automatically.
3
Get your signal in ~20 seconds
Claude Sonnet analyses everything and returns a Buy / Hold / Review research view with support & resistance levels, key risks and a full written rationale.
